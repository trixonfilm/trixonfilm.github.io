TRIXON FILMS

A Cinematic Studio of Storytellers

TRIXON FILMS is a boutique wedding photography and cinematography studio, born from a love of Indian weddings and the belief that every fleeting look deserves to be a keepsake forever.

We work with professional cinema cameras, cinematic prime lenses, drones and lighting — but our real craft is emotion. From haldi mornings to sangeet nights and grand receptions, we tell your story with the pace, color and soul of a feature film.

“Every Frame Matters. Every Story Lives Forever.”
